Enemy Faction Map

What each enemy faction is trying to do as a team: its plan, each unit's job in it, the combos between units and whether they really happen, the kill-order choice it gives you, and which hero themes answer it. Goal: factions that fight as one army, not five separate mobs. Unit texts come from the gamepedia (2026-11-08 export); numbers are placeholders. The judgments are Claude's first read from the code and the logs of one or two playtests, so check them in play.

What the grid says

    Ideas for faction teamwork

    Proposals, none built. Rules, kits and AI behaviour need the user's OK; the "Needs" line says what each touches. Faction-wide ideas first, then fixes for each faction's gap.

    Combo counters to log

    So "do they work as one?" becomes a number per fight. Built 2026-11-09: Core/ComboTally.cs counts them, battle logs save them as combos, and tools/dev.sh sim N combos writes the sim's numbers (shown on each faction's page).

    How enemies coordinate today

    From Core/EnemyAI.cs. Each enemy finishes its turn before the next starts, in the order they entered the battle, and picks its own target. Teamwork comes only from the pieces below.

    Roles

    A unit's job in its faction's plan. A unit can have two. A faction with a Setup and a Payoff has a combo; one with only Anchors and Fillers has HP.

    Criteria

    In order of weight. Each faction is scored good, ok or weak on each.
