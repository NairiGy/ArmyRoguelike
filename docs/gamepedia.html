Run & map

  • Draft 3 different base units. Army size up to 4.
  • Act 1 has 7 steps; each step offers 2 nodes. The four factions are shuffled onto four battle nodes (two at step 1, one at steps 3 and 5), so none repeats. Steps 4 and 6 offer a Trainer; the last step is a boss (Necromancer or Giant).
  • The other choices are shops and ? events.
  • Won battle: 10 gold + 5 per surviving unit (+5 for an ambush). Gold carried off by escaped cutpurses is lost.
  • Trainer: each unit can evolve one tier per visit. The first evolution of a visit is free, the second costs 20 gold, each after that 35.
  • Shop: 3 items and a random base-unit recruit for 15 gold (if the army has room).
  • Every battle starts at full HP and dead units come back. Evolutions, items, Bloodthirst damage and shrine HP last the whole run.

Battle rules

  • Moving costs 1 AP per tile (4 directions, no diagonals). A basic attack costs 2 AP. Up to 2 unused AP carry over.
  • Side phases: all your units act (one at a time), then all enemies.
  • Zone of control: each step out of a tile next to an enemy costs +2 AP, for both sides. Hit-and-run is possible but expensive.
  • Ranged attacks need line of sight (rocks block); Arcane Bolt ignores it. Bombs, boulders and fireballs are lobbed.
  • Mines: 4 damage (5 with Big Mines) to everyone in the 3x3, set off when an enemy steps on one. Bombs explode at the start of the next enemy turn. Blasts set each other off.
  • Push: a unit pushed into a rock, unit or the edge takes 2 damage. An enemy pushed onto a mine sets it off.
  • Poison: 2 damage at the start of the target's turn, 2 times. Burning ground: 1 damage at the start of the turn of whoever stands on it.
  • Snared units can't move on their next turn. Slowed units start their next turn with less AP.
  • Hover any enemy to see what it plans to do next.
