# Research: how similar games solve our problems (2026-11-08, research subagent)

Short version of a web-and-memory survey (Into the Breach, Slay the Spire, Monster Train, Darkest Dungeon,
Inscryption, Wildfrost, Fights in Tight Spaces, Balatro). What we took from it is marked **Used**.

## Late-game tension
- Into the Breach: the power grid never heals between islands, so the pressure lasts the whole run. Difficulty comes from spawns
  scaling with progress. Slay the Spire: each act brings new monster mechanics, and bosses check whether the build answers
  something. Darkest Dungeon: injuries last. Monster Train: the boss gets harder every floor, and the final boss punishes builds that don't scale.
- Ideas: **Cracks** (a statue that falls in battle is cracked, -max HP until a mason mends it) **Used**; boss phases with a cap of one
  phase per turn, so burst can't skip a boss **Used**; affixes on late fights **Used**; ascension-lite levels (later).

## Bosses on small boards
- The boss is a familiar enemy with one new rule (the ITB Psion) or a board-changing gimmick (Inscryption). Players remember the gimmick,
  not the HP. A final phase changes the board. A weak point that a push or a specific line exploits.

## Unit identity and synergy
- Slay the Spire: each character owns 1-2 keywords, plus rare build-defining powers. Monster Train: synergy where two clans' keywords meet.
- Ideas: one owned keyword per line that other lines read (Archer Marked, Rogue Exposed, Mage Slowed / Burning,
  Sapper Rubble) **Used in the builder briefs**; an "upgrade a skill" offer in the pick; a signature move at level 5.

## Map and events
- The best Slay the Spire events are clear trades with known costs (Golden Idol, Big Fish, Vampires). FTL and ITB show fight info up front, so the route is a real choice.
- Ideas: show more on the nodes (faction, battlefield, affix, reward type); events that cost something you own (a technique
  swap for a rare one, a crack for a level, a temporary recruit, a harder fight for double gold); an event answered by what you drafted.

## Identity rule candidates
- Statues harden when they don't act; **the roof crumbles** (pushes and heavy hits crack tiles that collapse, fallen units
  become rubble walls; the researcher's pick); night and dawn as a turn clock.

## Onboarding
- Staged unlocks over the first runs (one new system per run), a scripted first fight, keyword tooltips everywhere, and a one-time
  hint on each new node type.

Sources: MCV and Hardcore Gaming 101 on Into the Breach, the GDC 2019 Slay the Spire metrics talk, PCGamesN on the Slay the Spire 2 events, the Slay
the Spire wiki events list, a Steam discussion of Monster Train's Seraph, and the GDC Darkest Dungeon postmortem.
